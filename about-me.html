Background (Origin Story...?)

I'm honestly not sure what to say, because the intro covered pretty much most of it. But I feel like I need to have something in this section, plus I'm operating on an uncaffeinated midnight state of mind and the off chance that no one's going to get to this anyway, so here we go.

Prose, marketing, design, journalism, wildlife blogging, and now data & business. Interests have changed, but I suppose a communication thread is storytelling— creating some message, idea, world and communicating it to this world.
I love storytelling— not about me though.

Along with stories, I've always liked numbers and math. I was pretty set on studying statistics but not on how and where I wanted to apply it. Before college, I thought... ecology & wildlife biology. Shape-shifting creatues in the deepest seas, or myth-based flowers in rainforests beating with more life in a square mile than my whole town. Learning about exotic organisms living in seemingly unearthly ecosystems was like exploring new little worlds.

College

I think this is what draws me to data— stats & datasci. I despised the idea of becoming a coder in high school because it felt robotic. Data analytics feels open-ended. I like how I don't know what I'm going to find, that it's up to me to uncover that storyline that's most relevant or impactful or surprising.

I declared my majors on a whim, wanting to explore tech and theoretical math, and also supplementing with finance and economics. Now, I enjoy applying my analytical skills to real business contexts, creating real impact on companies, organizations, and communities. I take part in tech consulting projects and business case challenges at U-M, and I've had several meaningful opportunities working with diverse organizations outside of school.

I'm currently getting into the probability & math part of my class scheduling plan with background in DS&A (C++), statistical computing, and predictive modeling specific to management consulting.
